# Business Context

## Problem

Public socioeconomic data is freely available, but it is fragmented across APIs, inconsistently structured, and rarely ready for direct analysis. Analysts spend significant time locating, cleaning, and reconciling datasets before any insight can be produced.

## Why it matters

Decision-makers need reliable, comparable indicators across countries and years. Without standardization, comparisons are misleading and time-to-insight is long.

## Scope

This Proof of Concept demonstrates an automated pipeline that retrieves five World Bank indicators for five countries over 2015-2024, cleans and harmonizes them, validates quality, and publishes analytics-ready datasets. It is an independent portfolio project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouthShare or any other organization.
